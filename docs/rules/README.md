# Technical regulations (reference copy)

- `STEM_Racing_Professional_Class_Tech_Regs_2026-2027.pdf`: STEM Racing UAE Professional Class Technical
  Regulations 2026-2027 (58 pages, dated 19 October 2026), as supplied by the owner on 2026-11-28. This is the
  authority. `docs/RULES.md` is our map of it to the referee's checks.
- `...txt`: `pdftotext -layout` of the same, for searching. The figures are only in the PDF, and several rules are
  defined by their figures (wing span, clear air, halo views).

## Readings that matter for the wings (2026-11-28; pages are the PDF's)

- **T1.5 (p. 9):** a wing has a leading and a trailing edge across its minimum required span; it is defined by
  span, chord and thickness.
- **T8.6.1 (p. 38):** the front span is measured at right angles to the vertical reference plane, along the
  leading edge, trailing edge, top and bottom surface; the shortest counts. Where another part of the car
  intersects the span, the total is the sum of at most two segments of at least 25.0 mm each.
- **T8.7 (p. 40):** 5.0 mm clear air from the front wing to any other part, across its minimum span. It is checked
  with a 5 mm ball on a 2 mm rod, which allows fillet radii where the wing joins a support.
- **T9.5.1 (p. 42):** the rear span is measured the same way (shortest of LE, TE, top, bottom). "The rear wing must
  exist as a single, unbroken minimum span of 50.0 mm." The figures show the wing held at its ENDS, with the span
  measured between the end supports' inner faces.
  - Nothing in the text or figures says the wing's own support may join it inside the span without breaking it.
  - **Strict reading, adopted here:** any part joined to or passing through the rear wing breaks the span at that
    y. The wing needs ≥ 50.0 mm unbroken on all four lines between such joins.
  - The referee's earlier note ("its own pylon under it does not break it") is NOT supported. An under-wing pylon
    breaks the bottom-surface line.
- **T9.5.2 (p. 42):** at most two overlapping elements; chord 15.0–25.0 mm, measured parallel to the vertical
  reference plane and normal to the track, throughout the existing span.
- **T9.5.3 / T9.5.4 (p. 43):** thickness 2.0–6.0 mm; top-surface height deviation ≤ 15.0 mm along the minimum span.
  The T9.5.4 figure shows a centre arch passing UNDER the wing without touching it.
- **T9.6 (p. 44):** 5.0 mm clear air from the rear wing to any other part, across its existing span (ball on rod).
- **T9.4.1–T9.4.3 (p. 41):** the whole wing and any support behind Ref B; overhang ≤ 40.0 mm; height ≤ 65.0 mm.
