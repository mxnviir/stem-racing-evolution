At this car (no lead, so mass counts) the race-time gradient is **mass, not drag**: per unit step, mass term
27.5 ms, drag term 3.1 ms, and the race-time and drag descent directions are almost orthogonal (cosine -0.17).
A race-time step therefore tests the mass map and the race model, not the adjoint. The owner's first question is
whether drag-based optimisation works, so P1 gets a drag arm. P0's race-model partial was taken at 0.36 N (grad.py's
default) instead of the measured 0.467 N: dT/dD 0.178 vs 0.186 ms per mN (4 % low; `--d20` added). The P1
directions use P0's gradient as computed.

## Measurements

Drag: whole car = 2 x the half-car `drag_mN`. Race time: `simscale_layout.race_time(rec, D20, L20)`, each car's own
built mass (`rec.json`) and frame-k drag and lift. A comparison A vs B is paired by frame: Delta_k = A_k - B_k,
reported as the mean over k = 0, 1, with SE = max(SE from the block SEs, |Delta_0 - Delta_1| / 2).

## Marks (fixed now)

- **P1-T** (DESIGN.md's P1): T(Tm) < T(Tp) by >= 3 SE, and T(Tm) < T(R1) and T(Tm) < T(R2).
- **P1-D** (the drag arm): D(Dm) < D(Dp) by >= 3 SE, and D(Dm) < D(S1) and D(Dm) < D(S2).
- **Slopes** (reported, no mark): measured / predicted for Dm - B and Tm - B (the sidepod adjoint overstated size
  about 2x).
- **Provenance** (reported, no mark): the cosine between B_dafoam's drag gradient and P0's. Below 0.5 means the
  directions were taken on a materially different car, and P1-D is read with that caveat.

Reading: P1-D met: the coarse adjoint carries to the whole car for drag, and P2 uses it. P1-D missed: P2 runs as
random + recombination (DESIGN.md), and the drag adjoint is dropped for the whole car and reported so. P1-T tells
whether the mass map + race model step goes the right way; it does not by itself support the adjoint.
