| Rebuild | Built | tol 1.0 mm (kept) | tol 0.3 | tol 0.15 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| u_2 = +0.3 (top, near the front axle) | about +0.33 g at ABS (foam +0.113, nose +0.274 at PLA) | +0.53 g (+54 %) | +0.41 g | +0.34 g |
| u_30..44 = -0.3 (whole side outline) | -2.99 g (foam -1.98, nose -1.01) | -2.95 g (-1 %) | -2.46 g | -2.02 g (-32 %) |

No single tolerance fits both: the error depends on where the move lands (near the front axle the rule masks and the
milling remove most of what the drawn surface adds). Kept at 1.0 mm (right for broad moves); the mass term carries
an error of up to about +-50 % for local moves. Every step is built and its race time measured, so this affects which
directions are proposed, not what is accepted. The exact alternative, a mass gradient from +-h builds of each number
(no CFD, ~45 builds per gradient), is the planned option for P2 if P1 shows the mass term matters.
